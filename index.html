About

The Northeastern Marketing Consortium brings together marketing faculty and doctoral students from five universities in the Northeast for a one-day meeting of research presentations and discussion.

The 2026 meeting is sponsored by the Yale School of Management and will take place at MIT Sloan on Friday, October 23.
